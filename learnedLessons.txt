I discovered that when a function does something related to business rule , we shouldn't put it in utilities, otherwise in some layer called 
domain , here where we are to define our business rules , and the services just going to controll the flow to apply the business operation.
utility functions are just some generic functions that does nothing related to our business.
and it can be used in any part without knowing who is gonna use it.


There is also another lesson i learned in creating the achievements feature , we shouldn't use the submit controller as an example to save our achivements and process them
neither the services because they don't care about achivements, and notice that the response of the submission is not related to the
achievements at all , meaning the user will be satisfied without it...
all i mentioned are problem, the solution has a name , it is Event-Driven architecture !
when a submission is created , i would fire an event that leaves the flow entirly through an event emitter , the process will run 
in the background , then i will send the response via web sockets , which solves Single responsibility and when the achivements logic break , submissions
will work perfectly , also the user shouldn't wait additional time just to get the achievements calculated to get his submission result.
